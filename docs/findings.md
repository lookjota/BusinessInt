# Analysis findings — template

Dataset: **synthetic sales, 2025**. Never present figures as real business performance.

After running `scripts/analyze.py`, record:

1. Completed orders, revenue (BRL), average order value.
2. Top product and highest-revenue category.
3. Month with highest sales and month-over-month variation.
4. Best-performing region.
5. Limitations: synthetic data, simplified orders, excluded cancellations and returns.

Write business explanations tied to observed values, not conclusions invented in advance.
